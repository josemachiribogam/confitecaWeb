From Ecuador to the Universe

Founded in Ecuador in 1963 with our first brand Agogo, Confiteca began as a visionary company with a massive dream: to create moments of pure joy through exceptional confectionery that would conquer the world.

Today, we're a vertically integrated global manufacturer with facilities across Ecuador, Colombia, and Peru. Our innovative brands reach 40+ countries worldwide through 100K+ points of sale.

Mission: Spreading joy, one bite at a time.
