🎯 Our Commitment

CONFITECA C.A. is committed to maintaining the highest standards in all aspects of our operation, guaranteeing quality, safety, and responsibility in each of our processes.

🏢 Commitment Areas

👔

Management Commitment

Allocate the required economic, technological, and competent human resources to strengthen the company's Management Systems.

✅

Quality & Food Safety

Produce and distribute products with the highest quality and food safety standards, ensuring our customers consume a safe and quality product. We establish and review objectives and programs for proper FSMS compliance, guaranteeing compliance with legal and regulatory requirements.

🌿

Environment

Implement improvement methodologies for more sustainable production that guarantees better resource management and lower environmental impact.

🦺

Occupational Health & Safety

Develop actions to protect the life, health, and physical integrity of each and every one of the company's employees in the performance of their work activities.

🛡️

Security & Control BASC SCMS

Maintain the integrity of our processes regarding the prevention of corruption and bribery crimes, money laundering, among others. Comply with legal, regulatory, and applicable country requirements and organization standards.

❤️

Social Responsibility

The company is committed to working for the benefit of each worker to prevent labor abuse, discrimination, forced labor, and child exploitation.

📋 Additional Principles

  • We foster fluid internal and external communication with our customers and stakeholders to ensure continuous improvement in our products and processes.
  • We promote the training of our personnel, guaranteeing competence in relation to food safety.
  • Improve and contribute to continuous improvement through Management Systems that maintain standards and efficiency and effectiveness indices.
  • Promote security in the use of information technologies.
