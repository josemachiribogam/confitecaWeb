Our Sweet Brands

From gum to chocolate, lollipops to hard candies—our diverse brand family delivers joy across every category and generation with bold flavors and unforgettable experiences.

Plop!

Lollipops

Bright, bold, and full of joy. Plop! combines fruity flavor with a chewy gum center, giving every lick a surprise twist. Sweet, playful, and made to make you smile.

Agogo

Ball Gum

Born in 1964, Agogo turned color into happiness. Each bubble bursts with fun, flavor, and childhood nostalgia — a timeless icon that proves happiness really does come in colors.

Super Hyper

Sour Candy

Our most extreme brand, made for people who want challenges & look for wild flavors. Daring experiences that are impossible not to share.

Dr. Look

Novelty Candy

Meet the mad candy scientist. From the legendary Pelukloca cotton gum to other playful inventions, Dr. Look turns every bite into an experiment in fun, flavor, and imagination.

Tumix

Mint Gum

Freshness that never quits. Tumix is Ecuador’s favorite mint gum — a cool burst that keeps breath fresh and spirits lifted. Available in different refreshing flavors that bring confidence to every smile.

Kataboom

Liquid-Filled Gum

A bubble gum with a bang. Kataboom hides a liquid core that bursts with intense flavor — a little thrill in every chew. Once you pop one, you’ll want to boom again.

American

Chocolates & Caramels

Smooth, creamy, and proudly Ecuadorian. American brings together fine cocoa and indulgent fillings in bonbons and caramels made to savor — one bite, and you’ll taste the craft.

❄️

Artic

Mint Gum

Cool and refreshing — just like its name. Artic offers elegant mint dragées with long-lasting chill. A modern take on freshness that never goes out of style.

Jaazz

Menthol Candy

Smooth, bold, and full of rhythm. Jazz is the candy that cools and refreshes with mint, cherry, or eucalyptus notes — a hit for those who like a little edge in their sweetness.

Chocotin

Chocolate Cream Cups

Small cups, big smiles. Chocotin is pure spoonable joy — creamy chocolate made for kids and anyone who never stopped being one.

🌈

Melo

Hard Candy

Colorful, classic, and loved by all ages. Melo mixes bright fruit flavors and chewy textures into one happy assortment that keeps the candy aisle smiling.

🎈

Globopop

Lollipops & Merch

Big, bold fun inspired by Plop! festival vibes — playful balloon experiences that amplify every celebration with color and joy.

🕶️

Boogie

Lollipops

Retro-cool energy with modern flavor. Boogie delivers rhythmic sweetness and bold style made to move.

🍬

Muss

Gum & Candy

Soft chew, strong character. Muss brings satisfying textures and playful flavors you’ll want to keep close.

From Ecuador to the Universe

Founded in Ecuador in 1964 with our first brand Agogo, Confiteca began as a visionary company with a massive dream: to create moments of pure joy through exceptional confectionery that would conquer the world.

Today, we're a vertically integrated global manufacturer with facilities across Ecuador, Colombia, and Peru. Our innovative brands reach 40+ countries worldwide through 100K+ points of sale.

Mission: Spreading joy, one bite at a time.
